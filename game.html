How to Play

Goal

Win 2 rounds to win the match!

On your turn

  • Play 1 card from hand.
  • Activate Order abilities of cards played on a previous turn (optional).
  • When you end your turn, you draw a new card, up to a limit each round (indicated by a number on top of your deck)
  • Instead of playing, you may pass.

Passing

  • Pass only if you haven't played a card or activated an ability.
  • Once passed, you can't play any more cards or activate abilities for the rest of the round.
  • When you end your turn, you draw a new card, up to a limit each round
  • Once both players have passed, the round ends and scores are compared.

End of round

  • A round ends once both players have passed.
  • The player with more power wins the round.
  • Starting player wins ties.
  • All cards left in play are then discarded.

Starting a new Round

  • Refill back to 4 cards in hand.
  • The player who won the last round starts.
