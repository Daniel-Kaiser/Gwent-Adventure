How to Play

Goal

Win two of three rounds. Each round, whoever has the higher total power on the board when both players have passed wins that round.

Turns

  • On your turn, play a card from your hand and/or activate a ready Order ability, then end your turn.
  • You may pass instead, locking in your current board for the rest of the round -- but once you've used an Order this turn, you must still play a card.
  • Once both players have passed, the round ends and scores are compared.

Cards

  • Bronze, Silver and Gold cards all add their power to your total once played.
  • Some cards have a Play ability (triggers immediately) or an Order ability (usable once, starting the turn after it's played).
  • Special cards (like Scorch) aren't placed on the board -- their effect resolves on their own, then they go to the discard pile.

Between Rounds

Cards used stay in the discard pile -- they don't return to your hand or deck. Manage your cards carefully across all three rounds!
